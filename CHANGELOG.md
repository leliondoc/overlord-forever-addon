1.9.0

**Overlord Forever 1.9.0**

- **PvP Groups (new)**: the greyed Layer Jumper button of the panel becomes a group finder for the open world, also opened with `/ov groups`.
  - **Publish your group**: pick an objective (attack, defend or hunt), a place (a whole front, one of its points, an outpost, a keep, or anywhere), a size (5, 10, 20 or 40) and a minimum level. Your listing is shown to every Overlord player of your faction, each in their own language.
  - **Sign up in one click**: the leader invites you, automatically or after approving your request (the leader's choice, shown on the listing). You accept the game's own invitation yourself.
  - **The fullest groups on top**: the groups you can join come first, those of your continent before the others, and a group moves up as it fills. A click on a listing puts a waypoint on its point, outpost or keep.
  - **For leaders**: requests wait in the window and are counted on the panel button; a party gets a button to convert to a raid; the listing withdraws itself when you lose the lead, go away, or after an hour without a new member, and it comes back after a `/reload`.
  - **One accepted sign-up every 5 minutes** per character: this window is for playing together.
  - **Light on the network**: listings travel apart from the map and ranking synchronisation, a leader sends about one small message a minute, and captures always go first.
  - **Off in instances**, like the rest of the addon.
  - **Update required**: players on an older version do not see the listings.
- **A window in your faction's colours**: it opens beside the Overlord panel, at the same height.
- **Tutorial**: a new "PvP groups" section (page 3), in the 7 languages.
- **World map**: the curved names of the points stay on their circle while the map zooms, and the end of a zoom costs less.
- **/ov perf** also times the group finder, Most Wanted, the guild alerts and the network indicator.
