**Overlord Forever 2.5.5**

- Ranking, captures: a player's capture count often stayed behind on the other players' screens, and the best capturers were the most affected. Past about a dozen different captured zones the count was no longer passed on, and captures made while nobody around was exchanging with you never left your client (a second computer then showed an older count). The count now travels whatever the number of zones, each player tells its own count to the players it exchanges with, and the captures are compared at the start of a ranking exchange. Update required for your own captures to be told this way.
- Ranking, killing blows: the column filled very slowly. In a fight, the very moment killing blows are made, most of them were dropped on the way and never sent again. They now wait and leave once the fight calms down, a player's own killing blows are told like its captures, and they are compared between players even on a busy evening. They still come after the kills: expect them some minutes late after a big fight.
- Ranking, big fights: with more than about a hundred players fighting, the latest totals of some players were dropped while waiting to be passed on. They now wait their turn instead.
- Recent activity and the relay: on a busy evening the messages that give the size of the fights could take half of what a client relays for the others and push other messages out of the queue. They now have a share of their own. On such an evening the Recent activity panel can be later for players behind a very busy client; captures, keeps, outposts and the ranking keep their room.
- /ov relay shows what was put aside, if anything, and the new counters of the ranking and of the fight messages.
- Network and sync hardening.
