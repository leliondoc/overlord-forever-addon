2.3.0

**Overlord Forever 2.3.0**

- **Works without the Overlord channel.** While the game keeps refusing it, the addon passes captures, sieges, the ranking and Recent activity from player to player by whisper, and now does it much better:
  - players you exchanged with are remembered for a few days, so a character the member list does not show finds them again at login;
  - a character without the member list also greets players of its faction from this week's ranking;
  - your new kill total also reaches your guild mates on Overlord directly, not only at the next ranking exchange;
  - a brand-new player with nobody to exchange with (no member list, no guild mate or Battle.net friend on Overlord) greets a few players of their faction met in PvP until one answers;
  - fights (Recent activity) now travel hand to hand too, so every panel shows the same fights again (a fight received by several paths no longer crowds out a new one, and a fight's lower or older sizes are no longer passed on once a bigger one is known, so the size that matters gets through);
  - guild rampage alerts travel hand to hand as well (one per guild every 10 minutes), and are detected again without the channel;
  - a siege that ends is cleared where it was told, and a siege heard late no longer stays orange for minutes after it ended;
  - captures and sieges passed hand to hand now also reach the guilds of the players who receive them (within the same small guild share as before), so a siege started by a lone player is seen by many more people.
- **Maps catch up much better when whispers get lost**: a map that arrives with a missing piece asks only for that piece again, instead of waiting minutes for the next full map.
- **PvP Groups work again without the channel**: listings and withdrawals pass from hand to hand (also to characters the member list does not show, and to the leader's guild), an edited listing reaches everyone again, a withdrawal reaches every player who got the listing (even after a /reload), players waiting on a listing that is withdrawn or edited are told at once, and an invitation the game refuses is told to you in chat. If you raise the minimum level of your listing, players who signed up below it are no longer invited automatically (you can still invite them).
- Recent activity: a fight you report while whispers are short is sent again shortly instead of missing from the others' panels, also when it grows within a few seconds.
- **The ranking catches up much faster without the channel**: players exchange the parts that differ several at a time, and right after a change the next exchange comes sooner; once your kill total stops moving it reaches the rest of your contacts too (on the test bench the ranking settles 25 to 50 % sooner than in 2.2.1 without the channel, with no more whispers).
- **The ranking's player records use about half the memory** (class, race, faction and guild of every ranked player: about 2.5 MB saved with 3,000 players, 17 MB with 20,000), with no extra cost while you play; your saved data keeps its format.
- No more repeated "You're doing that too fast" from the addon: while the game refuses the Overlord channel, 2.2.1 tried to join it again every minute with a burst of channel joins and leaves. Now a refused channel is tried again with a single join, less and less often (up to every 15 minutes), never right after a zone change; in a group, the addon also paces its messages to the group instead of sending them at once.
- Chat addons that show every channel notice (Chattynator, for instance) no longer print "left channel OvPad..." and "not allowed in this channel" every minute while the Overlord channel is refused.
- Maps stay the same for everyone more reliably: every shared date now uses the server clock (a PC clock a few minutes off no longer changes what that player's map accepts), and a late siege message from an ally can no longer undo a newer enemy capture on one map only; the same goes for a late copy of an ally capture, and two messages about the same point now give the same map whatever order they arrive in.
- Ranking: beyond the 5,000 players shown, the header gives "#5000+" and your kills instead of "not ranked yet"; the fortress and outpost lists no longer rebuild in a loop when one of them fails, and a search no longer starts over while they load.
- Honorable kills after a battleground or a dungeon: the first kills back outside are no longer lost.
- Outposts and fortresses: a capture can no longer start and then be undone by itself; the "left the point" line no longer shows while you are mounted or dead on it; two guild mates holding the point together no longer count the capture twice; after a /reload, an assault you only watched no longer shows a holder nobody else sees; outnumbered on the point, a capture no longer starts and fails every second (with a chat line each time); a guild mate's capture left hours ago no longer resumes near its end; a fortress assault still under way can no longer be taken for an old one; defenders standing on their site no longer ask the whole network for the map every 22 s.
- Your data is saved at logout even if another part of the addon fails, and the weekly reset can no longer be missed for a week.
- Keep and outpost assault alerts now come at half the capture, and only if the assault is still going on: an enemy just passing through a keep or an outpost (a quest path, for instance) no longer sets off an alert. The map still shows the assault at once, and a defender standing on the site still sees enemies arrive.
- Keep and outpost alerts now only concern your guild (its sites attacked, its assaults, its captures); the assaults, defences and captures of other guilds stay on the map but no longer fill the chat. A new option, "Keep and outpost alerts of other guilds", brings them back.
- Mines: mining now needs PvP on, like captures (without it, no platinum and no alert; a reminder says so once a minute), and the enemy mining alert names the player and their layer, e.g. "Darrow Hill (Name, layer #12)".
- A capital taken by the other faction now shows its new owner's hall in the zone list and on the map (it kept its old owner's colour).
- /ov perf now gives a report like /ov network: a verdict per line (freezes, heaviest frame, average load, garbage, the addon's memory), the details below, in the copyable window. In /ov network, a long ranking catch-up that is still receiving pages is no longer flagged as waiting.
- /ov help: the /ov map line shows its options again.
- Fixed: in a raid, the same news no longer goes to the raid once per member; capture messages in chat can no longer go quiet for minutes on a busy evening; a victory waiting for proof no longer applies while you are in an instance; a ranking that could neither give nor take pages for a whole week (saves carried over from an earlier week) now does.
- After a battleground or a dungeon, the addon finds its contacts again within seconds instead of one player a minute.
- A whisper you send to a player who is offline shows the game's "No player named..." message again (the addon only hides the ones about its own whispers).
- **Update required**: on 2.2.1, hand-to-hand fights do not arrive, a siege started by a lone player reaches nobody, and maps repair less well.
