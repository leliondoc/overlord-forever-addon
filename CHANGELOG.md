2.0.4

**Overlord Forever 2.0.4**

- **Coins become Platinum**: same uses (mines, the featured front, Attack and Reinforce), a new name and the game's platinum bar icon, in every language.
- **Most Wanted becomes Nemesis**: the week's five best enemy killers keep their skull under a new name, in every language. Their alerts are switched on or off in the options only (the /ov wanted command is gone).
- **Platinum for a Nemesis**: your killing blow on one of the five Nemesis now earns +10 platinum, shown like a mine's gain (once per player per half hour, never in instances, up to the usual 100 cap).
- **Very large fights told apart**: recent fighting now shows 750+, 1000+, 1500+ and 2000+ HK above 500+. Players still on 2.0.3 or older do not know these brackets: for such fights they show 500+ or nothing at all, so update.
- **A quieter addon**:
  - keep and outpost captures no longer play the raid warning sound, whoever takes them;
  - when your guild's keep or outpost is attacked, a red line now shows in the middle of the screen, without sound, next to the chat line;
  - a total victory no longer plays music or a sound; its red line stays;
  - the General's duel music is off for now.
- **Sound settings respected**: with sound effects muted in the game's options, Overlord no longer plays its sounds through the master channel. The Overlord option that mutes its sounds now also mutes the click of the PvP Groups window and of the world map button.
- **Overlord Rank**: an "i" at the top right of the tab reminds you that your rank is saved on this computer only.
- **Colours**: a few stray colours brought back to the addon's palette; tutorial commands are now in gold.
