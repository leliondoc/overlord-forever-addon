**Overlord Forever 1.9.1**

- **PvP Groups**: smoother invitations when the game is slow to deliver them, a board that says when it is still listening for groups after a login, a chat line for the leader when a full party must become a raid to keep inviting, and a declined sign-up that shows how long to wait.
- **Call to arms**: the button now opens a horn in your faction's frame; the call is only sounded by a click on the horn, never by mistake.
- **Capture bar**: redrawn inside your faction's bar frame on the main panel.
- **Most Wanted**: a crown replaces the skull on nameplates, in the ranking and in the tutorial.
- **Ranking window**: a Horde versus Alliance illustration behind the lists.
- **Windows**: the ranking, the Hall of Fame and PvP Groups no longer show through each other where they overlap; the one opened or clicked last comes to the front.
- **Update recommended**: PvP Groups works best when everyone is on 1.9.1.
