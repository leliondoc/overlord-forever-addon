2.0.5

**Overlord Forever 2.0.5**

- **Top ranks seen by everyone**: from rank 11 (Dragon Lord, Warmaster, Crown Prince, Sovereign, Overlord), other players now see your rank badge next to your name, in the ranking and on nameplates, in your faction and in the other one. Each client checks a rank for itself before showing it, so a badge can take a few weeks of play to appear, and a rank once shown is kept.
- **Update required**: older versions neither announce nor show these badges.
- **Captures on the world map**: a point being taken keeps its defender's colour (grey for a neutral point) under stripes in the attacker's colour, with its orange edge.
- **New outpost in Feralas**: Anad'Ora Moonwell, near the entrance of Dire Maul, a guild outpost open all the time, like Lesi's Bear Cave in Darkshore. Players on 2.0.4 or older do not see it.
- **Mines on the compact map**: a bigger mine icon, sized like the capture points' icons, with the reserve written larger in an arc just above it.
- **Smoother maps**: map icons and faction emblems keep their place and size while the world map is zoomed or scrolled, also with world map addons that scroll freely, and the minimap circles follow the minimap's zoom right away.
- **Minimap button tooltip**: the front you are on, its points held by the Alliance, the Horde or nobody, and your rank with your Glory.
- **PvP Groups**: the faction emblem breathes while the search connects and listens for groups.
