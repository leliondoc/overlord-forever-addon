2.2.1

**Overlord Forever 2.2.1**

- **/ov network and /ov relay also open in a window**, easier to read than the chat: it scrolls, and a "Select all" button lets you copy the whole report (then Ctrl+C) to share it.
- The panel's **"Your rank" button becomes "Website"**: it gives the link to overlord-forever.com. Your Overlord Rank is still on its tab of the character sheet.
- At login, the addon now spaces out its channel joins and leaves, so the game says "You're doing that too fast" less often. (The same message when you cross a zone border comes from the game itself, with or without Overlord.)
- Network dot: while the game refuses the Overlord channel, the addon's tries to get it back no longer turn "Blizzard throttle" red; messages to your group that the game holds back show yellow at most (they are sent again).
- The network tooltip counts only the players of the member list who really answered you, and no longer repeats the same sentence.
- Plays alongside 2.2.0: nothing changes in what clients send each other.
