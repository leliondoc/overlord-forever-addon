**Overlord Forever 2.0.6**

- **Lighter on your computer**: the minimap circles now follow the pace you move at instead of being recalculated at every frame. They glide just as before, with far less work while walking or riding. With a rotating minimap they still follow every frame.
- **Rank badges on nameplates**: when news about a player's rank arrives, only that player's badge is redrawn, instead of every nameplate in sight once a second.
- **Overlord panel**: the list of the front's points is prepared once, instead of several times a second.
- **/ov perf**: the measure also shows its three heaviest moments and what ran in them.
- Plays alongside 2.0.5: nothing changes in what players exchange.
