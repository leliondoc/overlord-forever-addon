1.3.5

**Overlord Forever 1.3.5**

- New options page in the standard Blizzard style (Esc > Options > AddOns > Overlord), with sections and short tooltips. Pick the chat tab for Overlord messages by its name, or click "Create tab" to send them to their own "Overlord" tab that you can move or hide like any other.
- Visual refresh: animated capture bar with a spark, zones under capture pulse on the world map, faction crests on the domination bar, in chat alerts and in the leaderboard, podium and own-row highlight in the leaderboard, real close buttons and a short fade when panels open. BfA banners, the main header and the Hall of Fame are unchanged.
- Player races fill in much faster: each player now shares their own race with their kills.
- Smoother big fights: the ranking index no longer restarts on every kill, fewer repeated network messages, lighter friend list and unit scans.
- Cross-faction live totals are ready for launch crowds: when many players relay the same enemy total, only a few post it to the channel (no change with few relays, and a total is never lost).
