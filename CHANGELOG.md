2.5.4

**Overlord Forever 2.5.4**

- Battlegrounds, dungeons and raids: Overlord now takes its chat filters out while you are in an instance and puts them back when you leave. An error raised there by another addon ("attempted to iterate a table that cannot be accessed while tainted", thousands of times in a battleground) should no longer name Overlord.
- Ranking, race icons: while the game refuses the addon channel, a player's race often never reached the other players and the ranking showed no race icon for that player. Every kill total now carries it; the icons come back as players update and fight.
- Layers window: it stayed almost empty for most players while the addon channel is refused. It now reads the layers of the players your client exchanges with, keeps a layer for 10 minutes and shows its age ("#6843 (4 min)"), lists your own faction only, and drops a player who left the fronts or does not answer a request. The window no longer opens in an instance.
- Chat channel number: at login Overlord now waits for the game's own channels while a lower channel number is still free, so that its channel stops taking /1 ahead of General. If one of your numbers is free on purpose, the first login waits about 10 seconds and the next ones do not.
- Network and sync hardening.
