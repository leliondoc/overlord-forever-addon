2.5.3

**Overlord Forever 2.5.3**

- Layer number: after you change layer, the badge and the Layers window now follow the new layer on their own. The old number no longer stays until a /reload.
- Killing blows: your first killing blow after a login or a /reload is now counted, and each blow is dated at the moment of the kill. A group mate's killing blow is no longer counted as yours.
- Keeps and outposts: a map that kept an old guild of the same faction on a keep or outpost (Menethil keep, for example) now follows the guild that really holds it, without waiting for an enemy capture. Players still on 2.5.2 can keep the old guild: update required.
- Performance: smaller one-frame spikes when the ranking catches up, at the weekly close and when maps arrive; less memory churn around the Battle.net relay, the zone indicator and the Layers window.
- Network and sync hardening.
