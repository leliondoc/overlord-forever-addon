**Overlord Forever 1.1.10**

- Leaderboard catch-up asks the nearest peer first (your own Battle.net friend) instead of a distant enemy-faction player, skips peers that stopped answering for 10 minutes, and abandons a transfer that stalled for 90 seconds. Players with few bridges now receive the full ranking, including the other faction, in minutes instead of half an hour or never.
- A map snapshot is no longer rejected as a whole because one of its zones is older than yours: fresher zones are applied and older ones are skipped. Two players who each missed a different capture now converge.
- The periodic map catch-up always starts at login, and a lost capture is re-sent through the relay.
- A victory bonus that arrives before the domination totals is kept and applied later instead of being lost. Domination uses server time, so a badly set computer clock is no longer rejected, and a client whose weekly reset has not run yet no longer sends last week's domination.
- Less relay congestion: pages of an already incomplete map are no longer forwarded, and a full map is no longer requested from every newly heard player right after a valid one arrived.
- Hall of Fame: beta champions of the week of September 22, the top 5 players and top 5 guilds with their final leaderboard rank, in Alliance and Horde categories.
- A second forged row from the same player is removed from this week's ranking.
