**Overlord Forever 2.3.3**

- Layer numbers are now right: players standing together on the same layer see the same number (for example "layer #7075" in capture and mine alerts, and in the panel). Before, two players side by side could read different numbers.
- A layer is only believed once two different NPCs agree, and creatures belonging to players (pets, totems, summons) are no longer read.
- Players still on an older version keep sending the old numbers until they update.
