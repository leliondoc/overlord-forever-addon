1.2.1

**Overlord Forever 1.2.1**

- Call to arms reworked: one call every 4 hours for the whole faction, from an active front, with no nearby-enemy requirement. It now reaches every Overlord player of your faction on the realm channel (plus relay and group) and shows as a raid warning instead of a popup.
- Leaderboard catch-up: when a player's version is not known yet, the complete catch-up is tried first, and a player who answers but never sends anything is skipped after 90 seconds instead of 20 minutes.
- /ov network now starts with a colored summary (green, yellow, red) of Blizzard refusals, relay losses, catch-up and queues; non-zero losses are highlighted in the details.
- Cleanup: the old time-based domination messages are no longer received or relayed, and all remaining wood code and texts are gone. Less traffic and less memory.
- Fixed: a call to arms received in the first 10 minutes after starting the computer was ignored.
