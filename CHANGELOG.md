1.7.3

**Overlord Forever 1.7.3**

- **World map, three display modes**: Full (as before, the default), Compact (circles and icons only; a point's name appears under the mouse and while it is being taken) and Hidden. Switch them with the new round Overlord button in the corner of the world map, the map's Filters menu, the minimap tracking menu (Overlord submenu, with the minimap icons box), the options or `/ov map` (`/ov map full`, `compact` or `off`).
- **Quest-friendly map**: coin mine and forest circles only catch the mouse at their centre, so quest objectives and NPCs under them show their tooltip again. Point icons no longer grow past a set size when you zoom in.
- **Capturers and rivalries**: in the leaderboard, the scroll icon in the outposts header shows this week's top keep and outpost capturers, and which guild took sites from which ("A > B" and how many times). Built from the captures your client already has, no extra network traffic.
- **Domination bar**: hover it to see the rule, this week's front victories (total per faction and the latest ones) and the time left before the weekly reset.
- **Calmer chat**: no more line on every honorable kill. Stepping out of a circle, keep or outpost is announced only after 2 seconds outside, so walking along the edge no longer fills the chat.
- **Floating panels, simpler**: two options remain, the floating coins panel and the floating next objective. The top HUD mode and the tutorial book icon are gone (the Tutorial button of the panel opens the guide). The coins panel's close button simply turns the option off, and `/ov hud on`, `off` or `toggle` drives the same option. A panel you had set to Never or closed stays off.
- **Help and welcome**: the welcome message points to the panel's Tutorial button (minimap button or `/ov show`). `/ov` lists `/ov map` and `/ov network`; `/ov layer` is removed.
- **Tutorial**: updated in 7 languages (map modes, domination bar, capturers and rivalries, `/ov hud`).
