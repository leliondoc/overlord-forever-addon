2.6.0

**Overlord Forever 2.6.0**

- New front: Tanaris, the eighth front of the campaign. Ten points on two roads across the desert, the Alliance capital at Lost Rigger Cove, the Horde capital in Thistleshrub Valley, and an outpost at the Broken Pillar. Gnome against Goblin. The tutorial describes it in every language.
- Update required: the map now holds one more front, so players on 2.5.5 or older no longer exchange the map, the capture history or the keep and outpost history with players on 2.6.0, and Tanaris is unknown to them. Live captures of the other fronts and the ranking still pass between versions meanwhile.
- Smoothness: a round of fixes against the small stutters some players felt, at the end of a fight, while scrolling the ranking, when an action bar changes, while reading the faction channel's member list, and in dungeons and battlegrounds, where more of Overlord now stops completely.
- Chat: with a translation addon (Ukrainian for instance), the game's "no player named ... is currently playing" lines for Overlord's own whispers were no longer hidden and could repeat for minutes after a player logged off. They are hidden again whatever the language, and that player is no longer whispered.
- Network and sync hardening.
